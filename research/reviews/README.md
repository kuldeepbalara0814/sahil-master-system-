## Teeno me jo cheez common nikli (sabse bharose layak)
- DS apna 2-5 draw pehle wala number dobara aata hai (~1.4-1.55% vs 1%), 60 saal me toota nahi, dheere kamzor.
- FB/GB/GL random jaise; Naksha/copy/link random se behtar nahi (DS par bhi).
- "Kab se nahi aaya wo ab aayega" ulta hai; hot-10 sirf 95x par barely.
- 90 din se faisla nahi ho sakta (~5 saal). Sirf paper trading.
