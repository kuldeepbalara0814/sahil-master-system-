## Pic me kya hai (data se milaya, examples.csv me har cell)
- Ek hi mahina: **Nov 2026**. Pic ki row = Nov ki tarikh + 32 (row 35 = 3 Nov, row 50 = 18 Nov). Pic 18 Nov ko li gayi: us din sirf FB 44 aaya tha, baaki GB/GL/DS narangi khaali.
- Rang number ke nahi, **rashi (family)** ke hain:
  - **Peela, chain 1 (02 rashi: 02 07 20 25 52 57 70 75):** 4 Nov GL 02, 6 Nov FB 75, 7 Nov GL 57.
  - **Narangi, chain 1 (24 rashi: 24 29 42 47 74 79 92 97):** 7 Nov GB 24, 8 Nov DS 24, 10 Nov GL 29.
  - **Peela, chain 2 (03 rashi: 03 08 30 35 53 58 80 85):** 14 Nov DS 85, 15 Nov GB 85, 17 Nov DS 80.
  - **Narangi, chain 2 (02 rashi):** 15 Nov DS 57, 16 Nov GB 25, aur **18 Nov GB/GL/DS khaali narangi** = 3rd step ki jagah.
- Chain ka design: ek rashi chhote samay me **3 baar** aati hai. Din ke gap upar ki chaaron chain me **1 aur 2** hain (4,6,7 / 7,8,10 / 14,15,17 / 15,16,18), yaani 4 din ki khidki me din 1, [din 2 ya din 3], din 4.

## Aap ne kya kaha (maine apne shabdon me)
1. Is design me narangi 18 Nov ki row khaali isliye chhodi ki **57 ki family wapas aa sakti hai**, wahi design jitne din ke gap se upar ki chain me bana. Yaani 3rd step usi gap par aata hai jis gap par pehle ban chuki chain me aaya.
2. Aise design **har mahine family me bante hain** (1 se 6 baar, kabhi kam-zyada). Aap ka kehna: man lo 7 baar diya to shuru ke 2 baar pakka pass karega, 3rd fail, 4th pass ho jata hai. **Ye mujhe pura samajh nahi aaya** (neeche sawal).
3. Is pattern ka naam: **family pattern**.

## Mera check (aap ke kehne par): pichle 1 saal (21 Nov 2025 se 21 Nov 2026)
Design maine ye liya: koi bhi rashi (kisi bhi game me) din d aur din d+1 par aaye (ya d aur d+2), phir **din d+3 par wahi rashi** (kisi bhi game me) aaye to PASS.
- Har mahine me ye bante hain: **15 se 63 baar** (Nov 2025 adha mahina, baaki mahine 45-63). Aap ke "1 se 6" se bahut zyada, kyunki maine har rashi li. Pic me 24 me se sirf 4 rang me the.
- 3rd step aaya: 8-number rashi me **166/556 = 29.9%** (random 28.4%), jodi rashi (4 number) me **13/75 = 17.3%** (random 15.1%). Yaani random jaisa.
- Har mahine kam se kam kuch PASS aate hain (4 se 22 tak), par uska aadha hissa fail. Mahine ke andar pass/fail ka kram (jaise PPFFFPF) aap ke "pehle 2 pass, 3rd fail" jaisa mujhe saaf nahi dikha.
- 18 Nov ka pic wala 3rd step (narangi): 02 rashi me GB 57 aur DS 70 aaye = **PASS**.
Ye jaanch sirf pichle 1 saal par hai, aur sirf "dekho ki bante hain ya nahi" ke liye, parakh nahi.
