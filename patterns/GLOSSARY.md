# Shabdkosh: rashi, cross, jodi, half jodi (user ne sikhaya, 2026-11-21)

Ye mere samajhne ke liye hai. Software me bhi yahi niyam chalenge. Naam bolo to isi ka matlab.

## Niyam

- **Rashi (family)**: number ke har ank me 5 jodo (ya na jodo, 9+5=14 -> 4), aur ank aage-peeche (palat) karo. 01 ke liye: 01 10 15 51 60 06 65 56 (8 number).
- **Cross**: har ank ka 'ank, ank+5' jodi: 01 ke liye 16 aur 50, inke palat 61 aur 05 -> 16 61 50 05 (4 number). Kabhi kabhi kaam deta hai, jodi me alag hota hai.
- **Jodi (double)**: 11 22 33 ... 00. Inki family sirf 4 hoti hai (palat alag nahi): 11 -> 11 66 16 61; 22 -> 22 77 27 72. Inka cross nahi.
- **Half jodi**: ank ka antar 5 ho: 16 27 38 49 (aur 05, palat 61 72 83 94 50). Iski family bhi jodi wali 4 number ki hoti hai (16 -> 11 66 16 61). (user ne pakka kiya: 05 bhi half jodi hai).
- **Gap**: ek number kitne din ke antar se dobara aata hai (4,5,6,7,8,9,10 din). Pattern banane me ye dekha jata hai.

## Aur shabd (aap ke, 2026-11-22)
- **Standing**: ek hi game column me agle din (GB se GB...). Same number ho ya family ka number, dono standing repeat.
- **Cross**: agle din doosre column me. Ye aankh ka naam hai; machine ke liye "agle din koi bhi column". Zaroori nahi.
- **Step 1/2/3**: sirf ek hi (current) mahine ke. Do alag mahine me same design = mapping/naksha, step nahi.
- **Seedha / ulta**: seedha = date 1 se 30 ki taraf niche; ulta = 30 se 1 ki taraf upar.
- **With location / out of location (variation)**: pass design wale location par aaya / doosre location par aaya.
- **Jodi rashi**: 11, 22, 33, 44, 55, 66, 77, 88, 99, 00 ki 4-number wali rashi (jaise 11 16 61 66). 8-number wali ~90%, jodi rashi ~10%.
- **Baar**: weekday (Ravivar, Somvar ...). **Tokri**: us baar ko sabse zyada aaye 6 number (aur palat samet ~12 number). Software me Baar Filter highlight karta hai (hint).
